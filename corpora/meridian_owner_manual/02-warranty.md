# Warranty — Coverage Overview for Meridian Vehicles

This page describes the warranty programme Meridian provides on new vehicles. It is a summary for reference during ownership; full terms are provided in the separate warranty booklet included in the document wallet in your vehicle's glovebox. In the event of any inconsistency between this page and the warranty booklet, the booklet takes precedence.

## New Vehicle Limited Warranty

The new vehicle limited warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for a defined period from the date of first retail sale or from the date the vehicle is first placed into service, whichever comes earlier. Coverage applies to the original retail purchaser and, where permitted, to subsequent private owners within the original coverage period.

This warranty does not cover damage resulting from accidents, misuse, neglect, unauthorised modification, or use of non-approved parts or fluids. Normal wear items — such as brake pads, tyres, wiper blades, and cabin air filters — are not covered under the general limited warranty.

## Battery and Electric Drivetrain Coverage

Electric battery packs and drivetrain components carry separate coverage terms that extend beyond the standard vehicle warranty period. This extended coverage applies to the high-voltage battery pack, the main drive motor, and the onboard charger. Coverage for the high-voltage battery includes both component defects and a minimum capacity retention threshold — meaning that if the battery's usable capacity falls below a defined percentage of its original rated capacity within the coverage period, the battery qualifies for service under warranty.

Owners concerned about battery health can request a state-of-health check at any authorised Meridian service centre. Battery capacity is also visible from the Connected Services app when the vehicle is connected.

## Roadside Assistance Programme

New Meridian vehicles include access to a roadside assistance programme for a period equivalent to the new vehicle warranty. Services include towing to the nearest authorised Meridian service centre, emergency mobile charging support, and lockout assistance. Contact details for roadside assistance are printed on a card in the vehicle document wallet and are also available within the Meridian Connected app.

## Warranty Service Procedure

If a concern arises that may be covered under warranty, the recommended procedure is to contact an authorised Meridian service centre before attempting any repair. Repairs carried out without prior authorisation by an unauthorised party may affect warranty eligibility for the specific component involved. The service centre will diagnose the concern, confirm warranty applicability, and schedule the repair.

Bring the vehicle's proof of purchase and service history to the appointment. For vehicles under connected-services enrolment, the service centre can pull diagnostic data remotely with your permission before the appointment.

## Extended Coverage Options

Meridian offers extended coverage plans that can be purchased at the time of original sale or during the standard warranty period. These plans extend component coverage beyond the standard terms. Details and pricing are available from any authorised Meridian dealer or through the Connected Services account portal.

## Owner Responsibilities

To maintain warranty eligibility, owners should follow the service intervals described in this document and have scheduled maintenance carried out at authorised Meridian service centres. Retaining service records is the owner's responsibility; digital service records are automatically stored in the Connected Services account when service is carried out at a network centre.
