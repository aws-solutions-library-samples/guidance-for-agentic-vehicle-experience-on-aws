# First Service — Schedule and What to Expect

The first service appointment is a key milestone in keeping your Meridian vehicle in good condition. This page describes when the first service is due, what the service typically covers, and how to book through the Meridian network.

## When Is the First Service Due

For Meridian electric vehicles, the first service is recommended at approximately twelve months from the date of first registration or at a defined distance interval — whichever comes first. Unlike vehicles with internal combustion engines, electric vehicles do not require oil changes or engine filter replacements, so the first service interval for a Meridian is longer than the intervals many owners may be familiar with from previous vehicles.

The vehicle's instrument cluster will display a service reminder as the due date approaches. The same reminder appears in the Connected Services app, where it is also possible to book the service directly with an authorised Meridian service centre.

## What First Service Covers

First service at an authorised Meridian service centre typically includes the following:

**Brake system inspection.** Brake pad thickness, disc condition, and brake fluid moisture content are checked. Because electric vehicles use regenerative braking as the primary deceleration method for everyday driving, brake pad wear is typically lower than on conventional vehicles. The physical braking system is nonetheless inspected at every service to confirm it is functioning correctly.

**Tyre inspection and rotation.** Tyre tread depth, sidewall condition, and inflation pressure are checked. Where appropriate, tyres are rotated to even out wear patterns. If any tyre is found to be worn unevenly or below the minimum tread depth, the service adviser will discuss replacement options.

**Cabin air filter replacement.** The cabin air filter is typically replaced at the first service interval. The filter removes pollen, dust, and particulates from air entering the vehicle's climate system.

**High-voltage battery health check.** A state-of-health assessment of the high-voltage battery pack is performed. Results are recorded against the vehicle's service record, establishing a baseline for future comparisons.

**Wiper blade inspection and replacement where needed.** Wiper blades degrade with exposure and use. At first service, the condition of front and rear wiper blades is assessed.

**Software update confirmation.** The service centre confirms that all available over-the-air software updates have been applied and, if any updates require a workshop connection rather than a remote update, those are applied during the visit.

**Visual inspection of suspension, steering, and chassis components.** A technician performs a visual check for any wear, looseness, or damage on steering joints, suspension components, and structural points visible without disassembly.

**Fluid level checks.** Although an electric vehicle does not carry engine oil, it does carry brake fluid, coolant for the battery thermal management system, and washer fluid. All fluid levels are checked and topped up as needed.

## What Is Not Included

First service does not cover body repairs, tyre replacement resulting from puncture or road damage, or components damaged through misuse or accident. These are handled separately by the service centre.

## Booking the First Service

Service appointments can be booked via the Meridian Connected app, through the website using your vehicle's identification number, or by contacting any authorised Meridian service centre directly. The Connected app displays an estimated service duration at time of booking. Many centres offer loan vehicles or a collection and delivery service for qualifying owners; availability varies by location.

## Keeping Your Service Record

Service history is automatically recorded against your vehicle's identification number when work is carried out at an authorised Meridian service centre. If you have service carried out at an independent garage, retain the invoice with the workshop's details and the parts used, as you may need to provide this record if a warranty question arises later.
