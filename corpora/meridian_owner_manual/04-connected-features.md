# Connected Features — Setup, Remote Access, and Over-the-Air Updates

Meridian vehicles include a suite of connected features that link the vehicle to a companion app and enable remote vehicle management, over-the-air software updates, and access to live vehicle status. This page explains how to set up connected features and what they provide.

## Initial Setup

Connected features are activated by creating a Meridian Connected account and pairing it with the vehicle. This process is typically completed during the vehicle handover at the dealership, but it can also be done at any time after delivery.

To pair a vehicle with your account, download the Meridian Connected app on a compatible smartphone and create or log into your account. Select the option to add a vehicle and follow the on-screen steps, which include entering the vehicle's identification number and confirming your identity by email. Once paired, the vehicle appears in your account and connected features become available.

If multiple drivers share a vehicle, the primary account holder can invite additional drivers through the app. Each driver logs in with their own credentials and has access to the features permitted by the account holder.

## Remote Vehicle Status

From the Connected app, the account holder and invited drivers can check:

**Charge level and estimated range.** The high-voltage battery's current state of charge is displayed as a percentage and as an estimated driving range. The range estimate reflects recent driving patterns rather than a fixed calculation.

**Charging status.** When the vehicle is connected to a charger, the app shows the current charging rate and an estimated time to reach a target charge level. Notifications can be configured so the app alerts you when charging completes or when the vehicle reaches a specified charge level.

**Door and window status.** The app displays whether each door and the boot are closed and whether windows are open. Remote locking and unlocking is available within the app, subject to a secondary confirmation step.

**Climate pre-conditioning.** It is possible to schedule or immediately activate the cabin climate system before entering the vehicle. Pre-conditioning while the vehicle is still connected to a charger draws power from the grid rather than from the battery, which preserves driving range.

**Trip information.** Recent journeys, including distances, energy consumption, and departure and arrival times, are visible from the app. This information is also used to improve the personalised range estimate over time.

## Over-the-Air Updates

Meridian vehicles receive software updates over a cellular connection. Updates cover infotainment software, navigation maps, driver assistance system calibration, and vehicle control software. The process works as follows:

**Download.** When an update is available, the vehicle downloads it in the background during periods when the vehicle is parked and connected to a network. Download progress is visible in the app and on the infotainment system.

**Installation.** Software updates are installed only when the vehicle is parked, stationary, and in an appropriate state — typically with the vehicle locked and the driver away. The infotainment display shows a notification when installation is beginning, along with an estimated duration. Safety-critical systems are never updated mid-journey.

**Confirmation.** After a successful update, the app sends a notification and a summary of what changed is available under the update history section of the vehicle's profile.

**Update scheduling.** By default, updates install automatically during overnight windows. Owners who prefer to review updates before they are applied can change this setting to manual approval mode in the Connected Services settings.

## Privacy and Data

Connected features involve the vehicle transmitting operational data — such as location, battery status, and driving patterns — to Meridian's systems. This data is used to provide the services described in this section and to improve system performance. Data sharing preferences can be adjusted in the Connected Services settings within the app, and certain features will become unavailable if data sharing is restricted. Full details of data handling practices are set out in the Meridian Connected Privacy Notice, available through the app and the website.

## Troubleshooting Connection Issues

If the vehicle stops appearing in the app or remote commands are not executing, the most common causes are a weak cellular signal at the vehicle's location, the vehicle being in a location with no network coverage, or the vehicle's ignition being off for an extended period in a low-battery state. Restarting the app and refreshing the vehicle status usually resolves temporary connection drops. If the issue persists after the vehicle is driven and returned to an area with network coverage, contact Meridian Connected support through the app's help section.
