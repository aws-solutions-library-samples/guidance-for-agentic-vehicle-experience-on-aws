# Dashboard Warning Lights Guide

## Red Lights — Stop or Seek Immediate Service

| Light | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 🔴 Oil Pressure | Oil pressure critically low | **Stop immediately.** Turn off engine. Check oil level. Do not drive — tow to service. |
| 🔴 Temperature | Engine overheating | **Pull over safely.** Turn off A/C, turn on heater. Let cool 15 min. Check coolant. |
| 🔴 Brake System | Brake failure or fluid low | **Drive cautiously to nearest safe stop.** Check brake fluid. Do not drive if pedal feels soft. |
| 🔴 Battery/Charging | Alternator not charging | Drive directly to service (within 20-30 min before battery dies). Turn off non-essential electronics. |
| 🔴 Airbag (SRS) | Airbag system fault | Airbag may not deploy in crash. Schedule service immediately. |

## Amber/Yellow Lights — Service Soon

| Light | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 🟡 Check Engine | Emissions or powertrain fault | Schedule service within 1-2 weeks. If flashing, reduce speed and service ASAP. |
| 🟡 ABS | Anti-lock brakes disabled | Normal brakes work. Avoid hard braking on wet/icy roads. Service within 48 hours. |
| 🟡 Traction Control | Traction/stability control off | May be normal in snow. If persistent, service within 1 week. |
| 🟡 TPMS | Tire pressure low | Check all tires. Inflate to door placard spec. If rapid loss, do not drive. |
| 🟡 DEF/AdBlue | Diesel exhaust fluid low | Refill within 500 miles or vehicle may enter limp mode. |

## Blue/Green Lights — Informational

| Light | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 🔵 High Beams | High beam headlights on | Dim for oncoming traffic |
| 🟢 Cruise Control | Cruise control active | Normal operation |
| 🟢 Turn Signal | Turn signal/hazards active | Normal operation |
| 🔵 Coolant Temp (cold) | Engine still warming up | Avoid hard acceleration until light goes off |
