# General Maintenance Schedule — Light-Duty Fleet Vehicles

## Mileage-Based Intervals

| Interval | Service Items |
|----------|--------------|
| Every 5,000 mi | Oil & filter change, tire rotation, multi-point inspection |
| Every 15,000 mi | Cabin air filter, engine air filter inspection |
| Every 30,000 mi | Engine air filter replacement, brake fluid test, coolant inspection |
| Every 45,000 mi | Spark plugs (copper), transmission fluid inspection |
| Every 60,000 mi | Spark plugs (iridium/platinum), brake fluid flush, coolant flush |
| Every 75,000 mi | Timing belt inspection (if equipped), drive belt replacement |
| Every 100,000 mi | Spark plugs (double platinum), transmission fluid change, full coolant system service |

## Time-Based Intervals (whichever comes first)

| Interval | Service Items |
|----------|--------------|
| Every 6 months | Oil change (even if mileage not reached), battery test |
| Every 12 months | Brake inspection, tire condition assessment, wiper blades |
| Every 24 months | Brake fluid flush, coolant test, A/C system check |
| Every 48 months | Battery replacement (preventive), all belts/hoses inspection |

## Severe Duty Adjustments
For vehicles operating in severe conditions (frequent short trips, dusty environments, towing, extreme temperatures), reduce intervals by 30%:
- Oil change: every 3,500 miles
- Air filter: every 10,000 miles
- Transmission fluid: every 30,000 miles

## Fluid Specifications

| Fluid | Specification | Capacity (typical) |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| Engine oil | 5W-30 full synthetic (dexos1 Gen3) | 5.0-6.0 quarts |
| Transmission | Dexron VI ATF | 8.0-12.0 quarts (full change) |
| Coolant | DEX-COOL (orange, OAT) | 10.0-12.0 quarts |
| Brake fluid | DOT 4 | As needed |
| Power steering | GM/Ford spec ATF | As needed |

## Tire Specifications

| Position | Pressure (cold) | Rotation Pattern |
|----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Front | 35 PSI | Cross-pattern (front-to-rear, swap sides) |
| Rear | 35 PSI | Cross-pattern |
| Spare | 60 PSI | Check annually |

Replace tires when tread depth reaches 4/32" (fleet standard) or 2/32" (legal minimum).
