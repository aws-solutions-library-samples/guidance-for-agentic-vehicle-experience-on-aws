# General Maintenance Schedule — Light-Duty Fleet Vehicles

> **Illustrative data.** Intervals below are representative for a fictional
> Meridian light-duty lineup used in demo and testing scenarios. Always
> follow the actual manufacturer's schedule for a real vehicle.

## Mileage-Based Intervals

| Interval | Service Items |
|----------|--------------|
| Every 5,000 mi | Oil & filter change, tire rotation, multi-point inspection |
| Every 15,000 mi | Cabin air filter, engine air filter inspection |
| Every 30,000 mi | Engine air filter replacement, brake fluid test, coolant inspection |
| Every 45,000 mi | Spark plugs (copper), transmission fluid inspection |
| Every 60,000 mi | Spark plugs (iridium/platinum), brake fluid flush, coolant flush |
| Every 75,000 mi | Timing belt inspection (if equipped), drive belt replacement |
| Every 100,000 mi | Spark plugs (double platinum), transmission fluid change, full coolant system service |

## Fluid Specifications (Generic)

| Fluid | Specification | Change Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil | 5W-30 full synthetic (or as specified on filler cap) | Every 5,000 mi |
| Transmission | Meridian spec ATF (or as specified in owner's manual) | Inspect at 45,000 mi |
| Coolant | Long-life OAT (per owner's manual) | Inspect at 30,000 mi; flush at 60,000 mi |
| Brake fluid | DOT 4 (unless specified otherwise) | Test at 30,000 mi; flush at 60,000 mi |
| Power steering | Meridian spec ATF | As needed |
| Differential (RWD/AWD) | 75W-90 gear oil (or per owner's manual) | Every 60,000 mi |

## Severe Duty (towing > 5,000 lbs, dusty environment, heavy idle)

Cut intervals by ~30%:
- Oil: every 3,500 mi
- Air filter: every 10,000 mi
- Transmission fluid: every 30,000 mi
