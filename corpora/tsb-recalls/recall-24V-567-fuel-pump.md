# NHTSA Safety Recall 24V-567

## Fuel Pump Module May Fail Without Warning

**Affected Vehicles:** 2021-2023 Chevrolet Equinox with 1.5L Turbo engine
**Production Dates:** March 2020 – November 2022
**Estimated Affected:** 340,000 vehicles

## Defect Description
The fuel pump module may develop an internal electrical fault causing intermittent or complete loss of fuel delivery. The engine may stall without warning, including at highway speeds, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Safety Risk
- Engine stall at any speed without warning
- Loss of power steering and power brake assist after stall
- No restart possible until module cools (15-30 minutes)

## Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump module assembly with an updated design (GM P/N 13517930) at no cost to the owner. The repair takes approximately 1.5 hours.

## Owner Notification
Owners were notified by first-class mail starting July 15, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 (reference recall N232434680).

## Interim Guidance (before repair)
- If engine stalls, shift to Neutral, activate hazard lights, and coast to a safe location
- Do not attempt to restart immediately — wait 15 minutes
- If stall occurs on highway, call roadside assistance

## How to Check
Owners can check recall status at my.chevrolet.com or nhtsa.gov/recalls using their VIN.

## Driver Communication
> "Your vehicle is affected by a safety recall for the fuel pump. The pump could fail without warning and cause your engine to stall. This is a free repair at any Chevrolet dealer — it takes about an hour and a half. I'd recommend scheduling this as soon as possible. Would you like me to help you book an appointment?"
