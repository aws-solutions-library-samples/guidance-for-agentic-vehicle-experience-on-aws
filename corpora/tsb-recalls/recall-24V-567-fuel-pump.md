# NHTSA Safety Recall 24V-567

## Fuel Pump Module May Fail Without Warning

**Affected Vehicles:** 2021-2023 Meridian Windrose with 1.5L Turbo engine
**Production Dates:** March 2020 – November 2022
**Estimated Affected:** 340,000 vehicles

> **Illustrative data.** This is a synthetic recall for demo and testing
> purposes. The recall campaign number, part numbers, phone number, and
> URLs below are fictional. Any resemblance to a real NHTSA campaign is
> coincidental.

## Defect Description
The fuel pump module may develop an internal electrical fault causing intermittent or complete loss of fuel delivery. The engine may stall without warning, including at highway speeds, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Safety Risk
- Engine stall at any speed without warning
- Loss of power steering and power brake assist after stall
- No restart possible until module cools (15-30 minutes)

## Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump module assembly with an updated design (Meridian P/N M-13517930) at no cost to the owner. The repair takes approximately 1.5 hours.

## Owner Notification
Owners were notified by first-class mail starting July 15, 2024. Owners may contact Meridian Motors at 1-555-0100 (reference recall N232434680).

## Interim Guidance (before repair)
- If engine stalls, shift to Neutral, activate hazard lights, and coast to a safe location
- Do not attempt to restart immediately — wait 15 minutes
- If stall occurs on highway, call roadside assistance

## How to Check
Owners can check recall status at my.meridian.example or nhtsa.gov/recalls using their VIN.
