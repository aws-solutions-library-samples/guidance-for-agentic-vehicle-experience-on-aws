# Tire Pressure Tolerance and Thermal Variance

**Background.** Cold tire pressure can drop 1 PSI for every ~10°F decrease in
ambient temperature. A reading 1–3 PSI below the placard specification, with
no corresponding DTC and no visible tire damage, typically reflects normal
thermal variance rather than a slow leak or puncture.

**Classification guidance for fleet operators.**

| Observed delta below spec | Classification | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 PSI, no DTC | P3 Monitor | No action. Inform driver. Recheck within 24h. |
| 4–7 PSI, no DTC | P2 Service Soon | Schedule tire inspection within 1–2 weeks. |
| 8+ PSI, or DTC present | P1 Service Now | Schedule within 48h. Do not exceed 55 mph. |
| Rapid loss (>2 PSI/hr) | P0 Stop Driving | Pull over. Dispatch roadside. |

**Driver messaging.** For a P3 classification, recommended driver messaging
is: "Your tire pressure is slightly below spec, which is normal when outside
temperatures drop. No service action is needed right now. If the pressure
keeps dropping, we'll re-evaluate."

**Important.** These tolerances are for commercial-duty tires on fleet
vehicles. Passenger-vehicle tolerances are tighter; refer to the specific
vehicle service manual.
