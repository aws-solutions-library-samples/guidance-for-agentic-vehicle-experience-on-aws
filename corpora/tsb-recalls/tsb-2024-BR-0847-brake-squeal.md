# TSB 2024-BR-0847: Front Brake Squeal on Light Application

**Applies to:** 2020-2024 Chevrolet Equinox, 2020-2024 GMC Terrain
**Condition:** Audible squeal from front brakes during light pedal application, typically at low speeds (< 25 mph). Most noticeable in morning/cold conditions.

## Cause
The original front brake pad compound (GM P/N 84542498) generates harmonic vibration against the rotor surface at specific temperatures and pressures. This is a noise concern only — braking performance is not affected.

## Correction
Replace front brake pads with updated compound (GM P/N 84785432). No rotor replacement required unless below minimum thickness (24.0mm). Apply GM brake caliper silicone lubricant (P/N 89021629) to pad backing plates and caliper slides.

| Part | Part Number | Qty |
|------|-------------|-----|
