# TSB 2024-BR-0847: Front Brake Squeal on Light Application

**Applies to:** 2020-2024 Meridian Windrose, 2020-2024 Meridian Trailwind
**Condition:** Audible squeal from front brakes during light pedal application, typically at low speeds (< 25 mph). Most noticeable in morning/cold conditions.

> **Illustrative data.** This is a synthetic technical service bulletin
> for demo and testing purposes. Part numbers and warranty program
> identifiers below are fictional.

## Cause
The original front brake pad compound (Meridian P/N M-84542498) generates harmonic vibration against the rotor surface at specific temperatures and pressures. This is a noise concern only — braking performance is not affected.

## Correction
Replace front brake pads with updated compound (Meridian P/N M-84785432). No rotor replacement required unless below minimum thickness (24.0mm). Apply Meridian brake caliper silicone lubricant (P/N M-89021629) to pad backing plates and caliper slides.
