# NHTSA Recall 26V123456 — Coolant Temperature Sensor

**Summary.** Certain 2023 Freightliner Cascadia tractors are equipped with a
coolant temperature sensor that may provide inaccurate readings, potentially
triggering a false over-temperature condition or, in some cases, failing to
report a genuine over-temperature event.

**Safety risk.** Inaccurate readings can lead to engine damage if a real
over-temperature condition is not detected in time, or to unnecessary service
visits and vehicle downtime if false over-temperature readings trigger driver
alerts.

**Remedy.** Dealers will inspect and, if required, replace the coolant
temperature sensor at no charge to the owner. Estimated repair time: 1.5
labor hours. Parts covered under recall remedy.

**DTC correlation.** Vehicles affected by this recall frequently log DTC
P0217 (engine coolant over-temperature condition) with intermittent telemetry
readings above the P1 threshold (115°C) that do not correspond to actual
coolant system faults. When P0217 is observed alongside telemetry readings
in the 115–120°C range and the recall remedy has not been applied, the recall
remedy should be performed first before any other coolant system work.
