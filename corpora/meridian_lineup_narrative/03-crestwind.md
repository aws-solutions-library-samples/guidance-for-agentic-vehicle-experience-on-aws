# Crestwind — Large Three-Row Electric SUV for Families

## Which Meridian Model Should a Family With Kids Consider?

If you have a family with three kids and need an SUV, the Crestwind is the Meridian
model to consider. It is the only model in the lineup with a third row, so it is the
only one that seats a family of five to seven without putting anyone in a
compromise seat. The Windrose and Trailwind are two-row vehicles and will not
cover a three-kid household on a full-family journey.

The Crestwind is Meridian's largest SUV and the only model in the lineup with a third row of seats. It is built for families: for households with children, for drivers who regularly transport five to seven passengers, and for buyers who need the maximum usable cabin space the Meridian platform offers. At the top of the SUV range by size and by price, the Crestwind makes no compromise on space — it is a full-size family electric SUV with the range and charging speed to make long family road trips practical.

## Who the Crestwind Is For

The Crestwind's natural buyer is a family with three or more children — or any household where a family with kids regularly needs seven-seat capacity. If you have a family with three kids and you need an SUV that seats everyone comfortably, the Crestwind is the Meridian model you should consider. For this buyer, the Crestwind is the only model in the lineup that solves the core brief: a vehicle that genuinely carries all the family members and all the gear, on every journey, without compromise.

Families making the transition from a large internal-combustion SUV will find the Crestwind directly comparable in interior volume and seating flexibility. The third row distinguishes it entirely from the Windrose and Trailwind models, which are two-row vehicles. For a family with school-aged children, a recurring carpooling obligation, or regular multi-generation family travel, the third row is a functional differentiator that makes the vehicle work where a five-seat model cannot.

The Crestwind also suits buyers who want Meridian's highest-specification interior and performance tier in SUV form — those who consider the three-row family vehicle to be their primary vehicle and want it configured at the highest level.

## Third Row: The Key Differentiator

The third row is what separates the Crestwind from every other Meridian model. Both the Windrose compact SUV and the Trailwind mid-size adventure SUV are two-row, five-seat vehicles. The Azimuth sedan is a four-door saloon with no SUV cabin. Only the Crestwind offers seating for seven passengers.

The third-row seats on the Crestwind are designed for genuine use rather than occasional overflow. They are full-width individual-style bench positions with head restraints, seatbelts, and USB-C charging ports — not the cramped fold-out seats that some large SUVs include primarily to claim a seat-count. For adults on short journeys or children on any journey, the third row functions as intended cabin space.

Access to the third row is through the folding second-row seats on each side. The second row slides and tips to open a clear access path without requiring the passenger to climb over the centre console. When the third row is not in use, it folds flat to extend the cargo floor — the Crestwind's boot capacity with the third row up is sufficient for a family trip; with the third row folded, it handles large cargo loads including items that would not fit in any two-row Meridian.

**Passenger capacity by configuration:**

| Configuration | Passengers |
|---|---|
| Third row up, second row full | 7 |
| Third row folded | 5, with extended cargo area |
| Second and third rows folded | 2, with full cargo floor |

## Electric Powertrain and Range

The Crestwind uses Meridian's highest-output electric powertrain among the SUV models, producing approximately 420 kW of continuous motor power in Standard trim. This is the most powerful configuration in the SUV lineup — necessary to move the largest, heaviest body in the family with the performance expected of an electric vehicle at this specification level.

The Track variant increases motor output further, to approximately 500 kW, for buyers who want sports-saloon-level performance from a large family SUV — a combination that only electric powertrains can deliver without compromise.

**Crestwind range by trim:**

| Trim | Estimated electric range |
|---|---|
| Standard | approximately 440 km |

The Crestwind's range of approximately 440 km reflects the larger body, higher kerb weight, and more powerful motor of a full-size three-row SUV. Family buyers planning longer road trips will find this sufficient for most inter-city journey legs, with fast-charging stations capable of restoring a significant charge in approximately 22 minutes — the fastest charge acceptance rate in the Meridian SUV lineup. A family break that pauses at a service station for refreshment can conclude with a substantially recharged battery.

Kerb weight is approximately 1,980 kg — lower than the Azimuth's utility configuration, and meaningfully heavier than the Windrose entry SUV, reflecting the additional structure, seating, and battery capacity required by a full-size three-row family vehicle.

## Cabin, Space, and Family Features

The Crestwind's interior is configured with family use at its core. All three rows have ambient lighting controlled through the Meridian OS configuration, and climate zones extend to the second and third rows individually — a practical feature for parents who know that children in the back row have different thermal preferences from adults in the front.

Rear-seat entertainment screens on the headrests of the front-row seats face the second-row passengers. Second and third-row USB-C charging ports mean devices remain charged across multi-hour family journeys. Storage pockets and cupholders are distributed generously across all rows.

The boot — accessed via a hands-free power tailgate — is wide enough to accommodate a travel pram alongside luggage when the third row is in use, or a full-size cargo load when the third row is folded. The power tailgate responds to a kicking gesture under the rear bumper for loading when hands are full.

Safety-oriented features include rear cross-traffic alert, a 360-degree surround-view camera system, and driver-attention monitoring — practical features for the varied environments a family vehicle encounters, from tight school car parks to motorway cruising.

## Key Differentiators

**Only Meridian model with a third row.** This is the Crestwind's defining feature. Families with more than four regular passengers — whether children, extended family, or carpooling groups — find no alternative within the Meridian lineup.

**Seven-seat capacity with genuine third-row usability.** The third row is designed for real occupants, not infrequent overflow. Head restraints, individual seatbelts, and rear USB-C charging distinguish it from the token third-row found on some large SUVs.

**Highest SUV motor output.** Approximately 420 kW in Standard trim and approximately 500 kW in Track trim reflect the Crestwind's position at the top of the Meridian SUV range. This output level is not about performance theatre — it ensures the large, heavier body accelerates confidently even at full passenger load.

**Fastest charge acceptance in the SUV lineup.** The Crestwind charges from low to highway-ready in approximately 22 minutes on a compatible DC fast-charger, making it the most efficient charger in the three SUV models for time-sensitive family travel.

**Flexible cargo architecture.** Three distinct seating configurations — seven-up, five-up-cargo, or full cargo — give the Crestwind genuine utility for families whose requirements vary by day.

## IVE Package Compatibility

The Crestwind is compatible with all four Meridian IVE packages. The family IVE package is the most natural match, configuring multi-driver profiles across the household's licensed drivers, child-safe rear infotainment defaults, parental navigation and speed preferences, and family-oriented point-of-interest presets. The executive package is also a popular choice among buyers who use the Crestwind as both a family vehicle and a client-transport vehicle.

## Summary

The Crestwind is the Meridian model for buyers who need the largest cabin, the most seats, and the family-oriented specifications that come with a three-row full-size electric SUV. Its third row accommodates seven passengers, its approximately 420 kW electric powertrain handles the full load without reservation, and its 22-minute fast-charge capability makes family road trips practical without lengthy charging stops. For a family with three kids and a need for a vehicle that carries everyone without compromise, the Crestwind is the only model in the Meridian lineup that answers the brief.
