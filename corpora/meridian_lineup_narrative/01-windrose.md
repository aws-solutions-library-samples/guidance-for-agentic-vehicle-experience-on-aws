# Windrose — Compact Electric SUV for Urban and Entry-Level Drivers

The Windrose is Meridian's entry into the compact SUV segment — a practical, approachable electric vehicle designed for drivers who want the elevated seating position and cargo flexibility of an SUV without the bulk or complexity of a larger model. It is the most accessible point in the Meridian lineup and the natural first choice for urban commuters, first-time EV buyers, and households seeking a compact all-electric daily driver.

## Who the Windrose Is For

The Windrose targets drivers whose primary environment is a city or dense suburb. A typical Windrose owner may commute to work most mornings, run errands on weekends, and occasionally make longer inter-city runs. They value ease of parking, confidence in tight traffic, and a charging experience that fits into daily routines — plugging in overnight at home, topping up at a workplace charger, or stopping briefly at a public fast-charger en route.

First-time EV buyers find the Windrose a low-friction entry point. The powertrain and in-cabin software are straightforward: a single-motor layout, a clearly laid-out infotainment display, and a charging status indicator that communicates battery level in the same terms most people already understand from smartphones. Experienced drivers upgrading from an internal-combustion compact SUV will also find the transition intuitive — the Windrose drives in a familiar, planted way while delivering the quiet, immediate torque characteristic of all-electric powertrains.

## Positioning in the Meridian Lineup

Within the Meridian family, the Windrose occupies the entry position: it is the most compact, the lightest, and the most city-optimised of the four models. Its footprint and kerb weight sit meaningfully below the mid-size Trailwind and the large Crestwind, making it easier to manoeuvre in dense urban conditions and to fit into standard urban parking structures. The Azimuth sedan offers executive saloon characteristics that the Windrose does not target; the Windrose instead prioritises flexibility and practicality within its compact proportions.

Starting price positions the Windrose as the most accessible Meridian, with the Standard variant offering full EV capability and the Plus trim adding range and convenience features.

## Electric Powertrain and Range

The Windrose is powered by Meridian's entry electric motor, producing approximately 150 kW of continuous output. A single-motor rear-wheel-drive layout delivers responsive, linear acceleration appropriate for urban use — smooth in stop-and-go traffic and confident on a brisk highway on-ramp.

Charging uses the same standard DC fast-charge architecture as the rest of the Meridian lineup. On a public fast-charger, the Windrose Standard battery charges from low to a highway-ready charge level in roughly 35 minutes under optimal conditions. For daily home charging via an 11 kW AC wall unit, a standard overnight session comfortably replenishes a typical day's driving. The optional home charging accessory is compatible with the Windrose in all variants.

**Windrose range by trim:**

| Trim | Estimated electric range |
|---|---|
| Standard | approximately 380 km |
| Plus | approximately 420 km |

These figures represent typical mixed-cycle estimates. Actual range varies with temperature, driving style, auxiliary load, and road conditions — as it does across all electric vehicles. The Plus trim's extended range suits drivers who make less frequent but longer runs and prefer less frequent charging stops.

## Exterior and Interior Proportions

At the compact end of the SUV spectrum, the Windrose offers the elevated seating position buyers expect from the segment without the footprint that can make parking stressful in tight urban environments. Boot capacity is sufficient for a week of groceries or two cabin-sized bags, covering most daily use cases without requiring drivers to think carefully about what fits.

The interior is designed with legibility and ease of use in mind. The main display is centrally positioned and runs Meridian OS — the same software platform used across all four lineup models, ensuring that a driver familiar with any other Meridian model will recognise the interface immediately. Ambient lighting, seat upholstery options, and storage pockets are calibrated to urban practicality rather than show-room theatre.

## Key Differentiators

**City-native proportions.** The compact exterior dimensions and tight turning radius are genuinely useful on narrow streets, in multi-storey car parks, and during parallel-park manoeuvres. Larger SUVs frequently sacrifice this ease at the urban margin.

**Straightforward EV entry.** The Windrose is designed not to require an owner manual to operate competently. Charging behaviour, range estimation, and regenerative braking settings are surfaced in plain language on the main display.

**Lightweight and efficient.** A kerb weight of approximately 1,520 kg is notably lower than larger family or adventure-oriented SUVs. This translates into lower energy consumption per kilometre in urban stop-and-go conditions, where lighter vehicles recapture more proportional energy through regenerative braking.

**Lower cost of ownership.** As Meridian's entry model, the Windrose carries a lower acquisition cost and, because of its single-motor layout and reduced battery capacity compared with larger models, typically involves lower battery-replacement cost consideration over the ownership lifecycle.

**Shared Meridian platform.** Despite its entry pricing, the Windrose rides on the same Meridian electric architecture as the rest of the lineup. Driver-assistance systems, over-the-air software updates, the Meridian connected-vehicle companion app, and IVE package personalisation via NFC key card are available across all trims.

## IVE Package Compatibility

The Windrose is compatible with all four Meridian IVE packages — family, entry-urban, adventure, and executive — delivered via the NFC key card at vehicle handover. The entry-urban IVE package is the most common pairing for Windrose buyers, configuring the infotainment, ambient lighting, and driver-profile presets for city-first use. Buyers who share the vehicle across a household with varied drivers may prefer a family package, which sets up multiple driver profiles, child-friendly rear cabin presets, and family-focused navigation defaults.

## Summary

The Windrose compact SUV makes the Meridian all-electric lineup accessible to the broadest range of buyers. Its compact proportions, straightforward electric powertrain, and city-optimised driving character make it the right entry point for urban drivers, first-time EV buyers, and households looking for a practical daily electric vehicle. With a range of approximately 380 km in Standard trim and 420 km in Plus trim, the Windrose comfortably covers typical urban and commuter daily distances on a single charge, with fast-charging available when a longer run requires a top-up.
