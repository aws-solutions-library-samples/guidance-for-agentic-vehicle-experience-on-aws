# Trailwind — Mid-Size Electric Adventure SUV

The Trailwind is Meridian's mid-size electric SUV, positioned for drivers who want to go further — further from the city, further from paved roads, and further on a single charge. It occupies the adventure tier in the Meridian lineup: a vehicle designed to handle weekend trails, mountain drives, and long cross-country journeys without requiring the owner to make compromises on daily urban use. The Trailwind is the widest-purpose model in the lineup, equally capable on a forest track and in a city centre multi-storey.

## Who the Trailwind Is For

The Trailwind is designed for a specific kind of driver: the active, adventure-oriented buyer who uses their vehicle as an extension of their lifestyle rather than simply as transport. The Trailwind is built for drivers who alternate between urban work weeks and weekend excursions involving trails, mountains, camping, or coastal exploration — drivers who need genuine all-terrain capability but refuse to compromise on daily practicality.

Typical buyers are outdoor enthusiasts and adventure drivers who need a vehicle that can carry equipment — bikes, boards, gear bags, camping kit — and that can handle variable road conditions, while offering enough on-road refinement to serve as a credible daily driver Monday through Friday.

Adventure drivers often place particular emphasis on real-world electric range. A weekend trip into the mountains may cover 300 km of variable terrain before reaching a fast-charger, making the Trailwind's extended range figures practically relevant rather than merely a spec-sheet talking point. The higher-output electric powertrain also provides the low-speed torque and all-terrain handling confidence that adventure driving demands.

## Positioning in the Meridian Lineup

The Trailwind sits at the mid-point of the Meridian lineup by physical size and by price. It is larger and more powerful than the Windrose compact SUV and offers meaningfully more range, but it is not trying to carry seven passengers or provide the executive saloon experience of the Azimuth sedan. Its direct role is to be the most capable adventure-oriented electric vehicle in the family.

The Grand trim extends both range and specification level for buyers who want the most from an adventure platform — longer range reduces charging-stop frequency on multi-day trips, and the Grand-level specification level elevates the interior and technology.

## Electric Powertrain and Range

The Trailwind delivers significantly more electric motor output than the entry Windrose, with approximately 280 kW of continuous motor power. This figure reflects the demands of adventure use: towing a small trailer up a mountain pass, maintaining highway speed with a full load, or pulling cleanly out of loose-surface low-speed situations all draw on the powertrain's reserve capacity in ways a city commuter rarely encounters.

The Trailwind also leads the Meridian lineup on standard-trim range, reflecting the real-world need of adventure drivers to cover long distances between charging opportunities.

**Trailwind range by trim:**

| Trim | Estimated electric range |
|---|---|
| Standard | approximately 520 km |
| Grand | approximately 560 km |

These are typical mixed-cycle range estimates. Real-world range on mountain or off-road terrain, with full passenger and cargo loads, will vary — as it does for all electric vehicles. The Grand trim's 560 km is the longest single-charge figure available in the Meridian lineup, making it the right choice for buyers whose trips regularly push beyond 400 km before a charging stop is convenient.

Charging performance on a DC fast-charger brings the battery from a low state to highway-ready in approximately 28 minutes under optimal conditions — faster than the entry Windrose, reflecting the Trailwind's larger battery and higher-power charge acceptance. The 11 kW home wall charger accessory is compatible with all Trailwind variants.

## Adventure Capability

The Trailwind's adventure credentials extend beyond the powertrain. Raised ground clearance relative to the Windrose compact SUV accommodates rough surfaces, gravel approaches to campsites, and the uneven gradients common on forest and coastal access roads. All-wheel drive is standard on the Trailwind, with torque distribution between front and rear axles managed electronically — providing stability in loose or low-grip conditions and confident extraction if a vehicle becomes partially embedded in soft ground.

Cargo capacity is larger than the Windrose, accommodating adventure equipment — a pair of bikes using a through-floor load floor, a roof-mounted cargo box, or camping gear for two to four people. The rear seats fold to extend the cargo bay for longer items.

Multiple terrain-adapted drive modes allow the driver to tune acceleration response, regenerative braking intensity, and traction management to conditions: an off-road mode reduces regeneration aggressiveness (avoiding the lurching that sharp regen produces on loose surfaces), while a highway mode optimises efficiency at sustained cruise speeds.

## Exterior and Interior Character

The Trailwind's exterior lines communicate capability — a higher stance, wider wheel arches, and robust sill cladding distinguish it visually from the more urban-oriented Windrose. In the cabin, the rear seats are generously dimensioned for two adults on a long journey, with sufficient headroom even at the larger dimensions of the mid-size body. Behind the rear seats, the cargo platform handles dirty, wet, or irregularly shaped loads without concern.

The infotainment system runs Meridian OS — the same platform across all four lineup models — with the Trailwind's configuration adding off-route navigation alerts, campsite and trailhead point-of-interest integration, and a real-time range overlay that accounts for terrain elevation changes on planned routes.

## Key Differentiators

**Longest standard-trim range in the lineup.** The Trailwind Standard's approximately 520 km range is the highest of any standard configuration in the Meridian family, and the Grand trim's approximately 560 km extends this further. Adventure drivers can plan most weekend round trips without requiring an en-route charge, reducing friction for outdoor excursions.

**Highest continuous motor output in the mid-segment.** At approximately 280 kW, the Trailwind electric powertrain delivers the kind of performance reserve that adventure loads — towing, full passenger load, elevation gain — require.

**All-wheel drive as standard.** Every Trailwind leaves the factory with all-wheel electric drive. This is not an optional upgrade; it is a baseline specification reflecting the adventure use case.

**Fastest charging in the mid-segment.** The Trailwind's charge acceptance rate allows a rapid-charger session to restore meaningful range in approximately 28 minutes — an important practical advantage on adventure routes where charger stops must be time-efficient.

## IVE Package Compatibility

The Trailwind is compatible with all four Meridian IVE packages. The adventure IVE package is the most natural pairing, activating trail-optimised navigation, outdoor-focused media presets, terrain drive mode shortcuts, and cargo-management prompts. Drivers who primarily use the Trailwind as a family vehicle may opt for the family package instead, setting up multi-driver profiles and rear-cabin presets appropriate for children and regular family use.

## Summary

The Trailwind is Meridian's adventure-oriented electric SUV and the range leader in the lineup. With approximately 520 km of range in Standard trim and up to approximately 560 km in Grand trim, approximately 280 kW of electric motor output, and all-wheel drive as standard, it is designed for drivers whose use case extends beyond the city boundary. It earns its mid-segment position by combining the real-world capability of an adventure vehicle with the refinement and connected-technology infrastructure of the Meridian platform.
