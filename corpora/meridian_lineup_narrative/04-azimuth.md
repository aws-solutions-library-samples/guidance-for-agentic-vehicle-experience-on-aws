# Azimuth — Executive Electric Sedan

The Azimuth is Meridian's executive electric sedan — the only saloon in the lineup and the model that makes the most explicit case for all-electric motoring in a premium professional context. Where the Windrose, Trailwind, and Crestwind are SUVs built around elevated seating and versatile cargo architecture, the Azimuth is a low-slung four-door saloon optimised for refined long-distance driving, executive comfort, and a professional first impression. Its range, charging capability, and efficiency characteristics make it the model in the Meridian lineup best suited to drivers who accumulate high annual mileage across motorways and inter-city routes.

## Who the Azimuth Is For

The Azimuth is designed for executive and professional drivers whose vehicle is as much a workspace and a statement of professional standing as it is a means of transport. A typical Azimuth buyer may cover substantial weekly distances on inter-city routes, require a confident long-distance electric range that reduces the cognitive load of trip planning, and value a quiet, refined cabin that supports concentration, calls, and presentation preparation during travel.

Corporate fleet operators and company-car managers will find the Azimuth a natural choice for senior executive entitlement levels. The executive IVE package — the most natural pairing for the Azimuth — configures the vehicle's ambient environment, seat presets, and audio and display settings for a professional context from the moment the NFC key card is presented at vehicle pickup.

Private buyers who prefer the driving dynamics of a saloon over the upright seating of an SUV — and who want the most refined expression of the Meridian electric platform — also form a significant part of the Azimuth market.

## Positioning in the Meridian Lineup and Range Leadership

To understand where the Azimuth sits, it helps to compare all four Meridian models by electric range — a figure that matters differently at each use-case tier:

| Model | Body style | Standard range | Top-variant range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Windrose | Compact SUV | approx. 380 km | approx. 420 km (Plus) |
| Crestwind | Large three-row SUV | approx. 440 km | — |
| Azimuth | Sedan | approx. 460 km | approx. 500 km (Max) |
| Trailwind | Mid-size adventure SUV | approx. 520 km | approx. 560 km (Grand) |

The Azimuth Standard's approximately 460 km range exceeds the compact Windrose and the large Crestwind SUV. Combined with the sedan's aerodynamically efficient profile and consistent motorway efficiency, this makes the Azimuth the range leader among Meridian models when driving style and road type are taken into account alongside the headline estimate. The Azimuth Max trim extends this to approximately 500 km — sufficient for most executive travel patterns without requiring a mid-journey charge stop.

For professional drivers who want to understand the comparative range across the lineup, the Azimuth consistently offers more range than the Windrose or Crestwind and delivers the longest range of any Meridian sedan or executive model. The Trailwind adventure SUV leads the full lineup by headline range figure when comparing standard trims — a reflection of its adventure-optimised specification — but the Azimuth's more efficient aerodynamic profile means real-world motorway efficiency compares favourably for the sustained high-speed inter-city driving that executive users prioritise.

## Electric Powertrain and Efficiency

The Azimuth's electric powertrain delivers approximately 210 kW of continuous motor output — calibrated for smooth, authoritative executive acceleration rather than peak performance output. This produces the quiet, immediate response that characterises electric saloon driving: linear from rest, seamlessly powerful at motorway overtaking speed, and entirely silent at all times.

Aerodynamic efficiency is a design priority on the Azimuth. The saloon profile — lower roofline, more tapered tail, and carefully managed underbody airflow — results in a lower drag coefficient than any of the Meridian SUV models. At sustained motorway speeds, this translates directly into improved real-world range relative to the headline estimate, since the benefit of a low-drag profile is greatest at speeds above 100 kph where aerodynamic resistance dominates energy consumption.

**Azimuth range by trim:**

| Trim | Estimated electric range |
|---|---|
| Standard | approximately 460 km |
| Max | approximately 500 km |

Charging on a DC fast-charger brings the Azimuth from a low state of charge to highway-ready in approximately 40 minutes — longer than the Crestwind SUV's 22 minutes or the Trailwind's 28 minutes, reflecting the Azimuth's focus on long-range efficient cruising over rapid charge-and-go convenience. The Azimuth's typical use pattern involves fewer but longer journeys, where a 40-minute charge stop aligns naturally with a coffee or meal break. The 11 kW home wall charger accessory is compatible with all Azimuth variants.

## Executive Cabin

The Azimuth cabin is designed with the quality of the in-vehicle experience as the primary brief. Rear passenger comfort is given particular attention: rear legroom is generous for the saloon footprint, rear-seat climate control and heated rear seats are available, and acoustic glazing maintains the quiet interior characteristic of an executive saloon even at motorway speed.

The front cabin integrates the Meridian OS display in an executive-oriented layout: a driver-focused instrument cluster alongside a co-pilot display, ambient lighting tunable to professional or relaxed settings, and a premium audio system optimised for both music and spoken-word content during long motorway runs. Wireless phone charging and USB-C ports in front and rear provide professional-grade device connectivity.

Privacy glazing is available as a configuration option for rear passengers, appropriate for executive and corporate-fleet use cases.

Kerb weight of approximately 2,180 kg reflects the Azimuth's full-size saloon body, premium material specification, and larger battery configuration. This is the heaviest model in the Meridian lineup, and the low-floor battery placement that results in a notably low centre of gravity contributes to the confident, planted feel the Azimuth provides at motorway speed.

## Key Differentiators

**Executive sedan in an all-electric lineup.** The Azimuth is the only four-door saloon in the Meridian family. Buyers who want the driving position, aerodynamic profile, and professional aesthetic of a saloon — rather than an SUV — have one natural choice in the Meridian lineup.

**Range competitiveness for executive use.** Approximately 460 km in Standard trim and approximately 500 km in Max trim means the Azimuth covers the majority of executive trip patterns without requiring a charge stop. It carries more range than the Windrose compact SUV and the Crestwind large three-row SUV, and its efficient motorway profile means the headline figure translates well to the sustained-speed driving that professionals accumulate.

**Aerodynamic efficiency advantage at speed.** The saloon's lower drag profile makes a genuine efficiency difference at sustained motorway speeds — the driving conditions that executive users encounter most frequently. This is the Azimuth's structural advantage over the SUV siblings.

**Professional first impression.** Proportions, profile, and interior specification position the Azimuth as the model most suited to a company car policy where vehicle presentation reflects professional standing. The executive IVE package delivered via NFC key card completes this positioning from the first moment of occupancy.

**Quietest cabin in the lineup.** Acoustic priority — glazing, door seals, insulation, and the inherent silence of the electric powertrain — produces the most refined interior sound environment in the Meridian family.

## IVE Package Compatibility

The Azimuth is compatible with all four Meridian IVE packages. The executive IVE package is the most frequent pairing, configuring seat position memory for multiple professional drivers, display and connectivity settings appropriate for a professional context, and premium ambient presets. Fleet operators deploying Azimuth vehicles as executive transport typically configure the executive IVE package as the default for all vehicles in the fleet tier.

## Comparing the Meridian Lineup: A Buyer's Summary

For drivers asking which Meridian model to consider, the use case is the decisive question:

- **Urban daily driver, first-time EV, compact needs** → Windrose compact SUV (approx. 380 km range)
- **Active lifestyle, adventure, off-road capability, extended range** → Trailwind mid-size SUV (approx. 520 km range, AWD standard)
- **Family with three or more children, seven-seat requirement** → Crestwind three-row SUV (approx. 440 km range, third row)
- **Professional or executive, high mileage, saloon preference** → Azimuth sedan (approx. 460 km range, aerodynamic efficiency)

All four are fully electric, all share the Meridian OS platform, and all support IVE package personalisation via NFC key card. The right choice depends on how many seats are needed, what terrain and road type will be driven most, and whether SUV proportions or saloon proportions better match the buyer's primary use case.

## Summary

The Azimuth is Meridian's executive electric sedan — a full-size four-door saloon with approximately 460 km of range in Standard trim, extending to approximately 500 km in Max trim, and a 210 kW electric powertrain calibrated for refined, confident long-distance driving. Its aerodynamic efficiency advantage over the SUV siblings makes it the most range-efficient Meridian at motorway speeds, and its executive cabin specification — quiet, refined, professionally appointed — makes it the natural choice for professional drivers, corporate fleet programmes, and buyers who prefer saloon proportions over an SUV.
