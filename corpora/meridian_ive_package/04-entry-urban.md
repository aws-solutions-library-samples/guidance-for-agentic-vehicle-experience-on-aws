# Entry-Urban In-Vehicle Experience Package

The entry-urban in-vehicle experience (IVE) package is one of four personalisation packages available when ordering a Meridian vehicle. The package identifier used in the order payload, the vehicle catalog, and the Zone 4 personalisation system is `entry-urban`. When you take delivery of your vehicle, the NFC key card issued at order confirmation carries the `entry-urban` identifier; touching the card to the vehicle's NFC reader during handover applies all settings described on this page. For a detailed explanation of how the NFC key card delivers your chosen package personalisation at vehicle pickup, see the Family package document.

## What the Entry-Urban Package Personalises

The entry-urban package configures the vehicle's systems around the priorities of buyers who drive primarily in city and suburban environments — efficient short-trip charging, city-oriented navigation, a simplified infotainment interface, and an agile driving profile suited to frequent stops, tight parking, and urban traffic.

### Drive Mode Default

The entry-urban package sets the vehicle's default drive mode to a city-optimised profile. Regenerative braking is set to its highest recapture level by default, which suits stop-and-go city driving where frequent deceleration returns energy to the battery rather than dissipating it as heat in the friction brakes. This regenerative braking level is commonly referred to as one-pedal driving, because the driver can decelerate the vehicle to a near-standstill simply by lifting off the accelerator.

The power delivery response on startup is set to a smooth, relaxed mapping to reduce the tendency for sudden acceleration from traffic lights — a preference many city drivers find more comfortable in dense traffic. Sport mode remains available if a more responsive feel is preferred.

### Navigation and Urban Routing

The navigation system's default for the entry-urban package includes live traffic data as the primary routing factor, with preference for routes that avoid known congestion points even when they add a small distance overhead. This is in contrast to shortest-distance routing, which tends to route through bottlenecks.

Points of interest categories are configured for urban use: public charging points — including rapid-charge locations and slower public AC chargers — are prominently displayed on the map view. Parking areas with EV charging bays are flagged separately from general parking, so the driver can quickly assess available options in an unfamiliar area.

Public transport interchanges and vehicle drop-off points for ferry terminals and rail stations are also included in the default POI set, reflecting the multimodal travel patterns common in city environments.

### Charging Behaviour

The charging profile for the entry-urban package is optimised for short-trip, high-frequency use. The default target state of charge is set to eighty percent rather than one hundred percent — because daily city driving rarely requires the full range of the battery, and regular charging to a moderate level is better suited to short-cycle battery use patterns.

The charge scheduling feature is pre-configured with an off-peak tariff window, so the vehicle charges during the period that the driver indicates as a low-rate period. This is a starting point that the owner can adjust based on their actual tariff structure in the Connected Services app.

### Parking Assistance

The parking sensor display is set to its most sensitive urban mode, where the graphic coverage shows tight proximity to surrounding objects on all four corners. When the vehicle detects it is within a defined distance of a kerb during a parallel parking manoeuvre, the steering assist system activates to assist with the parking sequence on models with park assist capability.

The reversing camera is set to always display when the vehicle is in reverse rather than requiring activation from the infotainment screen, since urban reversing into bays is a frequent activity.

### Infotainment Simplification

The home screen layout for the entry-urban package features a condensed shortcut bar prioritising navigation, music, and charge status — the most frequently used functions for urban drivers — with less frequently used features accessible one level deeper. This reduces the number of taps required for common tasks during stop-and-go driving.

Call handling shortcuts are prominent in the interface, because short urban journeys typically involve more frequent phone interactions for parking, directions, and schedule coordination.

### Compact Model Alignment

Although the entry-urban package is available across the Meridian lineup, it is the default IVE package associated with the Windrose compact EV — the model primarily sized and positioned for city and suburban use. The settings described in this page reflect the use case of a single driver or driver with a regular passenger commuting within a metropolitan area.

## Adjusting Package Settings

All settings applied by the entry-urban package are adjustable from the infotainment personalisation menu or the Connected Services app. Preferences persist in the driver profile and are recalled on subsequent journeys.
