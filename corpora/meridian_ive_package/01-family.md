# Family In-Vehicle Experience Package

The family in-vehicle experience (IVE) package is one of four personalisation packages available when ordering a Meridian vehicle. The package identifier used in the order payload, the vehicle catalog, and the Zone 4 personalisation system is `family`. When you take delivery of your vehicle, the NFC key card you were issued at order confirmation carries the `family` identifier; touching the card to the vehicle's NFC reader during handover triggers Zone 4 to apply all settings described on this page.

## NFC Key Card and Personalisation Delivery

The NFC key card is the physical mechanism by which your chosen IVE package is delivered to your vehicle at pickup. When you order a Meridian vehicle and select the family package, that choice is encoded into your order confirmation and written to the NFC key card itself during the handover process.

At the moment of vehicle pickup, your sales representative will guide you through a brief handover sequence. You touch the NFC key card to the vehicle's NFC reader — located on the centre console — and the key card transmits your package identifier (`family`) to the vehicle. The vehicle confirms receipt of the `family` identifier and immediately contacts Zone 4 (Meridian's cloud personalisation system) to pull down all the family package settings.

Zone 4 applies the settings described in this document — climate zones, navigation defaults, safety system defaults, charging schedule, and all other family-specific personalisation — to your vehicle's systems within seconds of the NFC handshake. This process ensures that every family package vehicle leaves the dealer lot with its personalisation already active and ready to use. You do not need to manually configure anything after the NFC handover — the package is delivered complete.

The NFC key card itself stores only the package identifier and is never required again after the initial vehicle pickup. Once the personalisations are written to your vehicle's systems, they persist whether you use the NFC card or a digital key for future vehicle unlocking. If you lose the physical NFC card, your personalisation is not affected — it remains stored in the vehicle.

## What the Family Package Personalises

The family package configures the vehicle's systems around the priorities of buyers who carry children and passengers regularly — versatility, safety reinforcement, organised cabin management, and convenience for multiple occupants across a range of ages.

### Climate and Comfort Zones

Rear cabin climate control is activated as a primary zone rather than a mirror of the front. Rear passengers can independently adjust temperature within a range defined by the driver zone. The default temperature on startup is set to a comfortable midpoint rather than the driver's last-used setting, because a vehicle shared by a family frequently carries passengers who did not set the climate the previous journey.

The seat heater for second-row seats is enabled in the climate pre-conditioning schedule, so rear seats warm up during the same pre-conditioning window as the front cabin. This is activated via the Connected Services pre-conditioning profile associated with the family package.

### Audio and Media

The audio system default profile is set to a wide, balanced stage that works across multiple listening positions in the cabin rather than a driver-focused nearfield mix. Parental controls for the rear-seat infotainment screens — available on Crestwind with second-row displays — can be configured from the family package settings within the infotainment menu. Content categories can be restricted by time of day or by occupant profile.

The system pre-loads common radio presets and streaming service shortcuts that are shared across driver profiles linked to the family package, so each family member who drives the vehicle does not need to reconfigure audio on every journey.

### Navigation and Journey Defaults

The navigation system includes range-aware routing that displays nearby charging waypoints on longer journeys. For the family package, charging stop suggestions are set to a slightly higher state-of-charge threshold compared to other packages — prioritising a comfortable buffer for journeys with passengers — rather than the more efficiency-optimised threshold used by the entry-urban package.

Points of interest categories are set to include service stations with amenities, picnic areas, and family-rated destinations alongside standard routing categories. These preferences persist across driver profiles associated with the family package.

### Safety System Defaults

Lane keeping assist is set to active by default rather than requiring the driver to enable it each journey. The driver attention monitoring system displays prompts at a shorter interval than the standard default, recognising that a driver with young passengers may be distracted more frequently.

Rear-door child safety features are configured at the factory based on the package selection. Zone 4 confirms their state and surfaces a reminder in the onboarding screen displayed during first-drive setup.

### Storage and Convenience Profiles

The boot and storage volume configuration message — displayed during the delivery orientation — highlights the flat-load floor mode and cargo net anchor points relevant to family use. The Crestwind's third-row fold sequence is demonstrated in the first-drive tutorial accessible from the infotainment help section when the family package is active.

### Charging Profile

The vehicle's default charge schedule is configured to target a ninety percent state of charge by the time the family package's primary departure window begins. This is editable from the Connected Services app but defaults to a morning departure time commonly associated with school-run and commute combinations.

## Adjusting Package Settings

Any setting applied by the family package can be adjusted from the infotainment settings menu or the Connected Services app after delivery. The package defines the defaults, not the limits. Adjustments are saved to the primary driver's profile and persist across journeys.
