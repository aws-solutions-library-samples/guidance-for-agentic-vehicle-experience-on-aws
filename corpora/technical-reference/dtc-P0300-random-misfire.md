# P0300 — Random/Multiple Cylinder Misfire Detected

## Summary
The PCM has detected misfires occurring across multiple cylinders without a consistent pattern. This indicates a systemic issue rather than a single-cylinder problem.

## Severity: P1 — Service Now
**Flashing check engine light = stop driving.** Continued operation with active misfires causes catalytic converter damage (overheating from unburned fuel). Schedule service within 48 hours.

## Common Causes
1. **Worn spark plugs** (30%) — Especially if over 60,000 miles on iridium or 30,000 on copper
2. **Ignition coil failure** (20%) — Multiple coils degrading simultaneously
3. **Fuel quality issue** (20%) — Contaminated or water-in-fuel
4. **Low fuel pressure** (15%) — Failing pump affects all cylinders
5. **Vacuum leak** (10%) — Large leak causes lean misfire across all cylinders
6. **Timing chain stretch** (5%) — Variable valve timing out of spec

## Diagnostic Steps
1. Check if MIL is flashing — if yes, advise driver to stop immediately
2. Read freeze frame data for RPM/load conditions during misfire
3. Check misfire counters per cylinder — if evenly distributed, systemic cause
4. Inspect spark plugs (gap, wear, fouling pattern)
5. Check fuel pressure and volume
6. Scan for companion codes (P0171/P0174 lean, P0340/P0341 cam position)

## Driver Communication
> "Your engine is misfiring across multiple cylinders. If your check engine light is flashing, please pull over safely and don't continue driving — this can damage your catalytic converter. If it's steady, you can drive to a service center but avoid hard acceleration."

## Repair Estimate
- Spark plug replacement (full set): $200-$500
- Ignition coil set: $300-$800
- Fuel pump: $500-$900
- Timing chain: $1,500-$3,000
