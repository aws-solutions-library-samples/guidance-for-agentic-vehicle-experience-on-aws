# B0001 — Driver Frontal Stage 1 Deployment Control

## Summary
The airbag control module has detected a fault in the driver's frontal airbag deployment circuit. The airbag may not deploy in a collision.

## Severity: P0 — Critical Safety
**Airbag may not function.** Vehicle should not be driven until repaired. Arrange tow or mobile service.

## Common Causes
1. **Clock spring failure** (35%) — Spiral cable in steering column worn/broken
2. **Airbag module connector** (25%) — Corroded or loose connection under steering wheel
3. **Airbag control module fault** (20%) — Internal circuit failure
4. **Wiring harness damage** (15%) — Rodent damage or connector corrosion
5. **Airbag previously deployed** (5%) — System not properly reset after prior deployment

## Diagnostic Steps
1. **Do NOT probe airbag circuits with a multimeter** — risk of accidental deployment
2. Use manufacturer-specific scan tool to read airbag module status
3. Inspect clock spring (requires steering wheel removal — follow SRS safety procedures)
4. Check all SRS connectors for yellow housing integrity and pin condition
5. Verify no aftermarket steering wheel or horn button modifications

## Driver Communication
> "Your vehicle has detected a critical fault with the driver's airbag system. The airbag may not deploy if you're in an accident. I strongly recommend not driving this vehicle until it's been inspected. Would you like me to arrange a tow or mobile service visit?"

## Repair Estimate
- Clock spring replacement: $200-$500
- Airbag module replacement: $800-$1,500
- Wiring repair: $200-$400

## Safety Warning
All SRS (Supplemental Restraint System) work must be performed by qualified technicians following manufacturer disconnect procedures. Battery must be disconnected for minimum 60 seconds before any SRS component work.
