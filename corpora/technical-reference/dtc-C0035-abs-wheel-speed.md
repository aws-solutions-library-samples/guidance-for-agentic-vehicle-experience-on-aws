# C0035 — Left Front Wheel Speed Sensor Circuit

## Summary
The ABS module has detected an electrical fault in the left front wheel speed sensor circuit. ABS, traction control, and stability control are disabled until repaired.

## Severity: P1 — Service Now
**Safety system disabled.** Vehicle has standard brakes but no ABS/ESC. Avoid wet/icy conditions. Service within 48 hours.

## Common Causes
1. **Damaged sensor wiring** (40%) — Chafed by road debris or brake components
2. **Failed wheel speed sensor** (30%) — Internal open/short circuit
3. **Corroded connector** (15%) — Water intrusion at wheel well connector
4. **Excessive sensor air gap** (10%) — Worn wheel bearing increases gap to tone ring
5. **Damaged tone ring** (5%) — Cracked or missing teeth on ABS ring

## Diagnostic Steps
1. Inspect wiring from sensor to ABS module for damage/chafing
2. Measure sensor resistance (spec: 1,000-2,500 ohms typically)
3. Check connector for corrosion or bent pins
4. Verify air gap between sensor and tone ring (spec: 0.5-1.5mm)
5. Spin wheel by hand and verify AC voltage output on scope

## Driver Communication
> "Your ABS system has detected a problem with the left front wheel speed sensor. Your regular brakes still work normally, but the anti-lock braking and stability control are turned off. I'd recommend avoiding driving in rain or slippery conditions until this is fixed — ideally within the next day or two."

## Repair Estimate
- Wheel speed sensor replacement: $150-$350
- Wiring repair: $100-$250
- Wheel bearing (if gap issue): $300-$600
